Pick a language from the menu. The transcript scrolls with the video and jumps to any line you click.

          
Chapters and scrub previews
Chapter marks sit on the timeline, and a thumbnail follows your pointer while you scrub.

Any logo file, wordmark or symbol
Upload an SVG or a PNG. Long wordmarks get their symbol cut out for the small controls, and dark logos sit on a white plate so they never disappear.
